    Project Overview & Abstract

    The Intelligent Healthcare Recommendation System uses data mining and machine learning to analyze patient symptoms, physiological vitals, medical history, and treatment records stored in normalized relational databases.

    Key Data Mining Methodologies Implemented:

    • Supervised Multi-Classifier (Random Forest, Naive Bayes, Decision Tree, KNN): Learns symptom-disease diagnostic mapping to provide differential diagnosis with confidence intervals.
    • Association Rule Mining (Apriori Algorithm): Uncovers co-occurrence patterns across symptoms and past treatment records (`Support`, `Confidence`, `Lift`).
    • Unsupervised Clustering (K-Means): Groups patient populations into 4 health risk personas based on vital metrics (BP, glucose, cholesterol, BMI).
    • Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS): Recommends line-of-treatment medications, dosage guidelines, contraindication alerts, and preventive care.
